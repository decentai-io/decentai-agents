# Sidra Office Supplies — travel and expense policy

Effective 1 January 2026. Fictional; for trying the Expenses agent.

## 1. Claims

Any expense above 50 USD needs a receipt. Claims are submitted within
30 days of the last receipt, in USD, with the trip and the customer or
purpose named.

## 2. Accommodation

Hotels are reimbursed up to 600 USD per night. Extras charged to the
room (minibar, laundry, films) are not reimbursed.

## 3. Meals

Meals are reimbursed up to 150 USD per day. Alcohol is not reimbursed.
Meals with a customer are reimbursed in full when the customer and the
purpose are named on the claim.

## 4. Transport

Taxis are reimbursed up to 120 USD per trip. Flights are booked by the
office; a flight bought personally needs the office manager's approval
before the trip.
